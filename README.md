# I don't trust willpower. So I built a system that makes quitting impossible.

I'm Maajith, and I build systems designed to reduce reliance on motivation through friction, accountability, and real-world consequences.
Most productivity tools remind you. CommitT *forces* you.

---

## CommitT

An accountability app that enforces real consequences for inaction.
No streaks. No reminders. If you fail, the system responds.

---

**Example 1 — You want to actually stick to the gym this time.**

You open CommitT and set it up once:

> **Habit:** Go to the gym
> **Schedule:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday — 6 AM to 7 AM
> **Location:** Your gym
> **Penalty:** ₹500 deducted. Or your most embarrassing photo sent to your best friend.
> **Penalty Waiver:** Miss a day? You chose upfront that escaping costs 400 captchas. No captchas, no waiver.
> **App Block:** Instagram, YouTube, and anything you pick — automatically blocked the moment your phone detects you've entered the gym. Walk out, they unlock.
> **Strict Mode:** Your choice at setup. By default you can edit, modify, or delete the task freely. Turn Strict Mode ON and the task becomes untouchable — no deletion, no uninstall, no tampering — until the task is fully complete.

Monday 6 AM hits. Your phone buzzes. You have to physically be at the gym to start the session.
You walk in — Instagram disappears. YouTube disappears. Every distraction, gone.
A random alarm fires at 6:34 AM — you have 60 seconds to verify you're still there.
You try to delete the commitment because you're tired. The system rejects it.
You leave at 6:45 AM. ₹500 gone before you reach your car.

---

**Example 2 — You have an exam in a week and you need to lock in.**

You open CommitT and set it up once:

> **Habit:** Study at the library
> **Schedule:** Every day this week — 5 PM to 9 PM
> **Location:** Your college library
> **Penalty:** ₹1000 deducted. Or a message sent to your accountability partner every time you fail.
> **Penalty Waiver:** Need a day off? You chose upfront that a waiver costs 600 captchas. Solve them all or the penalty stands.
> **App Block:** Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat, anything you pick — automatically blocked the moment your phone detects you've entered the library. Only your study apps remain.
> **Strict Mode:** Your choice at setup. Leave it off and you can still edit the task. Turn it ON and nothing can touch it — no deletion, no uninstall, no way out — until the week is done.

5 PM hits. You have to physically walk into the library to start.
You sit down — every distraction locks itself. Only your study apps remain.
A random alarm fires at 7:12 PM — verify you're still there or the penalty triggers immediately.
You think about leaving early. You remember the ₹1000. You open your notes instead.
Exam week ends. You studied every single day. Not because you were motivated. Because you made leaving harder than staying.

---

That's not a reminder app. That's a consequence engine.

- **Set the schedule** — pick the days, time, and exact location
- **Set real stakes** — money penalties or an embarrassing photo sent automatically on failure
- **Waiver on your terms** — you decide upfront how many captchas it costs to escape a penalty
- **Location-triggered app blocking** — distracting apps lock the moment you walk in, unlock the moment you leave
- **Forced presence** — location check-in required to start, random alarms confirm you stayed
- **Strict Mode is your choice** — off by default, fully editable. Turn it on and the task is locked until completion. No deletions. No uninstall. No way out.
- **Immutable rules** — backend locks your penalty the moment you commit, you can't lower your own stakes mid-session
- **Hard deadline waivers** — even the forgiveness system has limits and can't be spoofed

---

## Why I Built It

Willpower failed me every time. So I stopped relying on it.

I cobbled together app blockers to force myself to the library and it worked —
not because I got more disciplined, but because I made quitting harder than continuing.
CommitT is that idea, fully built out.

I use it every day. For the gym. For shipping code. It's the reason this project exists.

I've documented every single day of building this — you can check it at [committ.mintlify.app](https://committ.mintlify.app)
