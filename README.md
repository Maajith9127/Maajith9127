## <p style="font-size: 1.8rem; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: -0.03em; color: #f0f6fc;">The Core Problem: Willpower Does Not Scale</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">We’ve all seen the loop. You look in the mirror or check your metrics and tell yourself you need discipline. So you attempt to build a strict routine—like trying to hit the gym daily or optimizing execution grids for exams. For two weeks, your execution is flawless.</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">Then internal motivation hits an inevitable trough. A lazy day slips in, a single friction point breaks your focus, and the entire routine collapses. You end up right back at square one, pulling an all-nighter or missing training sessions, frustrated that you broke another baseline promise to yourself.</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">Relying on simple human motivation to maintain high performance is a foundational architectural flaw. Motivation experiences downtime; systems do not.</p>

---

## <p style="font-size: 1.8rem; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: -0.03em; color: #f0f6fc;">The Engineered Solution</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">I stopped trying to patch my internal discipline with willpower and made an aggressive optimization call: offload compliance to hardware and OS-level fail-safes that keep the runtime active when motivation fails.</p>

> # <p style="font-size: 1.8rem; font-weight: 800; color: #58a6ff; line-height: 1.4; letter-spacing: -0.04em;">"Factor out human hesitation entirely. Build an unyielding digital guardrail that forces compliance at the system level."</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">Over the last 6 months, I engineered and deployed that exact infrastructure: <b>CommitT</b>.</p>

---

## <p style="font-size: 1.8rem; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: -0.03em; color: #f0f6fc;">What CommitT Does Under the Hood</p>

* <span style="font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 700; color: #f0f6fc;">Hard-Coded App Blocking:</span> <span style="font-size: 1.15rem; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">Uses custom Kotlin Accessibility Service tripwires to instantly kill access to distracting platforms based on active time and location metrics.</span>
* <span style="font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 700; color: #f0f6fc;">Settings Lockdown (Strict Mode):</span> <span style="font-size: 1.15rem; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">During active commitment blocks, the application hard-locks system settings and rule modifiers to completely reject user bypass attempts.</span>
* <span style="font-size: 1.25rem; font-weight: 700; color: #f0f6fc;">Concurrent Write-Gates:</span> <span style="font-size: 1.15rem; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">Engineered with a serialized SQLite global transaction layer to ensure local system rules evaluate with zero database deadlocks.</span>
