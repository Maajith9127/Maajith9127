## <p style="font-size: 1.8rem; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: -0.03em; color: #f0f6fc;">The Problem: Willpower is a Lie</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">We’ve all been stuck in the same loop. You promise yourself you're going to stay disciplined—whether it's trying to hit the gym every single day or studying consistently so you aren't stressing before exams. For two weeks, you do great. Then motivation fades, a lazy day slips in, and your new habit completely vanishes. You're right back at square one.</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">Relying on simple willpower to change your habits doesn't work because motivation runs out. I got tired of constantly breaking promises to myself, so I stopped trying to rely on motivation and decided to build a solution.</p>

---

## <p style="font-size: 1.8rem; font-weight: 700; letter-spacing: -0.03em; color: #f0f6fc;">The Solution: CommitT</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">Instead of hoping I'd stay motivated, I built a system that forces me to follow through. Over the last 6 months, I built an app called <b>CommitT</b>.</p>

> # <p style="font-size: 1.8rem; font-weight: 800; color: #58a6ff; line-height: 1.4; letter-spacing: -0.04em;">"Don't rely on willpower. Build a digital guardrail that physically won't let you quit."</p>

### <p style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.6; font-weight: 400; color: #8b949e;">It locks things down on your phone so that when your motivation inevitably drops, your own code steps in and holds you accountable. When others quit because they don't feel like it, this system keeps you moving forward.</p>
